Thanksgiving · Thursday, November 26

Let AI help with Thanksgiving.

Keep the traditions. Let AI do the busywork. Thirteen things to ask, with the exact words to copy.

Short answer: Open any free AI helper (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini), copy one of the requests below, change the details, and press Enter. It answers in seconds.

See it work, start to finish

Example: when to thaw the turkey

  1. Tap the green "Ask ChatGPT" button below. ChatGPT opens in a new tab with the question already typed in. No account needed.
  2. Change the details to fit you. Your turkey's weight, your dinner time.
  3. Send it. Tap the arrow, or press Enter. Wait a few seconds.
Your questionI have a 16-pound frozen turkey and we eat Thursday at 3 p.m. When do I move it to the fridge, and what if I forgot?

What you'll get back (a sample; yours will be worded a little differently):

Move it to the fridge by Sunday morning. A 16-pound turkey needs about 4 days in the refrigerator, about 1 day for every 4 to 5 pounds. Leave it in its wrapper, on a tray, on the bottom shelf. Starting a day early is fine: a thawed turkey keeps 1 to 2 days in the fridge.

If you forgot: thaw it in cold water. Keep it wrapped, cover it with cold tap water, and change the water every 30 minutes. Allow about 30 minutes per pound, so about 8 hours. Then cook it right away. For a 3 p.m. dinner, start very early Thursday morning.

Last resort: you can cook it frozen. It's safe, but it takes about 50% longer. Never thaw it on the counter.

Then keep talking to it, like a person. Try: "Make me a reminder list for Sunday through Thursday." Sample checked against USDA turkey guidance.

In the kitchen

Grandma's recipe card[Take a photo of the handwritten card and upload it.] Type out this recipe clearly, and make it for 12 people.
Cooking timelineWe're eating at 3 p.m. Make me a cooking timeline for a 16-pound turkey, stuffing, mashed potatoes and two pies, with one oven.
LeftoversI have leftover turkey, stuffing and cranberry sauce. Give me 3 easy recipes, with short ingredient lists.
A guest who can't eat glutenOne guest can't eat gluten. Which of these dishes are safe, and how can I change the others? [list your dishes]

At the table

A grace or toastWrite a short Thanksgiving grace for our family. Warm, simple, and under one minute to read.
Table talkGive me 10 easy, kind conversation questions for a Thanksgiving table with ages 8 to 85.
Family memoriesHelp me write a one-page story about my first Thanksgiving as a kid, from these notes: [your notes]. Keep my words where you can.

With the grandkids

GamesSuggest 5 indoor games for kids ages 6 to 12 after Thanksgiving dinner. No screens, nothing to buy.
Thank-you notesHelp my grandkids write short thank-you notes to the cook. Give 3 simple examples they can copy.

After the holiday

Black Friday scam checkI got this text about a Black Friday deal or a package delivery: [paste it]. Is it a scam? What should I do? Don't tell me to click any link.
Medicare deadlineMedicare open enrollment ends December 7. Explain in plain words what I should compare before then.

AI can make mistakes. Check cooking times and food safety with a trusted source, and never type in account numbers or passwords.

Common questions

Can AI really read Grandma's handwritten recipe card?
Usually, yes. Take a clear photo in good light, upload it to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and ask it to type the recipe out. Check the result against the card.

Which AI should I use for Thanksgiving planning?
Any of the free big three works: ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai) or Google Gemini (gemini.google.com).
