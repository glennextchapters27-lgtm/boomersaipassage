For kids and grandkids

Short answer: Our top pick is Claude. Asks you questions, then shapes your answers into chapters in your own voice.

Our picks, best first

1

Claude

Best writing partner

Asks you questions, then shapes your answers into chapters in your own voice.

Easy to use ●●●●○ · Value ●●●●○

Free version. Pro about $20 a month. Made by Anthropic.

How to start
  1. Create a Project in Claude called "My Story".
  2. Ask it to interview you, one question at a time.
  3. Every week, ask it to turn answers into a chapter.
Try saying this:Interview me about my childhood, one question at a time, so we can turn it into a story for my grandkids.
