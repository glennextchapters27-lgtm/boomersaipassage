Build a tiny app without coding

"Vibe coding" for beginners

Short answer: Our top pick is Claude. Describe what you want in plain words and Claude builds a working page you can click on, right in the chat.

Our picks, best first

1

Claude

Best first step

Describe what you want in plain words and Claude builds a working page you can click on, right in the chat.

Easy to use ●●●●○ · Value ●●●●○

Free version. Pro about $20 a month. Made by Anthropic.

How to start
  1. Go to claude.ai.
  2. Describe the tool in a few sentences.
  3. Ask for changes until it's right.
Try saying this:Make me a simple page to track my blood pressure readings each morning, with a chart of the last 30 days.
Open Claude
2

Lovable

Best for a real website

Builds a full website or app from a description and can put it online.

Easy to use ●●●○○ · Value ●●●○○

Free version with daily limits. Paid plans available. Made by Lovable.

How to start
  1. Make a free account at lovable.dev.
  2. Describe your app.
  3. Click to publish when you like it.
Try saying this:Build a website for our neighborhood garden club with a calendar and a sign-up form.
