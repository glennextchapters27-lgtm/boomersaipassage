Our picks, best first

Merlin Bird ID

Best for birds

Free from Cornell. Point your phone at a singing bird and it names it by sound.

Easy to use ●●●●● · Value ●●●●●

Free. Made by Cornell Lab of Ornithology.

How to start
  1. Install Merlin Bird ID.
  2. Tap Sound ID and hold up your phone.
  3. Watch the names appear.

Google Lens

Best for plants and everything else

Take a picture of almost anything and Google tells you what it is.

Easy to use ●●●●● · Value ●●●●●

Free. Built into the Google app and many Android cameras. Made by Google.

How to start
  1. Open the Google app.
  2. Tap the camera icon.
  3. Point it at the plant.
