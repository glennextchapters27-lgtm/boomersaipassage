Fix a phone or computer problem

Step-by-step tech help

Short answer: Our top pick is Google Gemini. Gemini Live lets you point your camera or share your screen and ask what to tap.

Our picks, best first

1

Google Gemini

Best for showing the problem

Gemini Live lets you point your camera or share your screen and ask what to tap.

Easy to use ●●●●○ · Value ●●●●●

Free version. Paid plans from about $5 a month. Made by Google.

How to start
  1. Open the Gemini app.
  2. Turn on Live and share your camera or screen.
  3. Ask: what do I tap next?
Try saying this:My printer says it's offline. Walk me through fixing it one step at a time.
Open Google Gemini
2

ChatGPT

Best with a screenshot

Take a screenshot of the error and ask what it means.

Easy to use ●●●●● · Value ●●●●○

Free version. Paid plans from about $8 a month; Plus about $20. Made by OpenAI.

How to start
  1. Take a screenshot of the problem.
  2. Upload it to ChatGPT.
  3. Ask for one step at a time.
Try saying this:Here's a screenshot of the error. Tell me what to do, one step at a time, and wait for me.
Open ChatGPT
3

Microsoft Copilot

Best for Windows PCs

Built into Windows and knows its settings well.

Easy to use ●●●●○ · Value ●●●●○

Free version. Included with Microsoft 365 plans from about $10 a month. Made by Microsoft.

How to start
  1. Click the Copilot icon on your Windows taskbar.
  2. Describe the problem.
Try saying this:How do I make the text bigger on my Windows laptop?
Open Microsoft Copilot
