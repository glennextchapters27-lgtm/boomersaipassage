Plan a trip

Itinerary, packing, what to see

Short answer: Our top pick is ChatGPT. Builds a day-by-day plan around your pace, budget and walking limits.

Our picks, best first

1

ChatGPT

Best trip planner

Builds a day-by-day plan around your pace, budget and walking limits.

Easy to use ●●●●● · Value ●●●●○

Free version. Paid plans from about $8 a month; Plus about $20. Made by OpenAI.

How to start
  1. Say where, when, who's coming and your pace.
  2. Ask for a day-by-day plan.
  3. Ask follow-ups: "less walking", "cheaper hotels".
Try saying this:Plan 4 relaxed days in Savannah for two people in our 60s. Not too much walking, good seafood, one day trip.
Open ChatGPT
2

Perplexity

Best for current facts

Shows the websites it used, so you can check hours, prices and closures.

Easy to use ●●●●○ · Value ●●●●○

Free version. Pro about $20 a month. Made by Perplexity.

How to start
  1. Ask a specific question.
  2. Click the source links to confirm.
Try saying this:Is the Biltmore Estate open the week of Christmas, and what do tickets cost?
Open Perplexity
3

Google Gemini

Best with Google Maps

Can pull in Google Maps and Flights information.

Easy to use ●●●●○ · Value ●●●●●

Free version. Paid plans from about $5 a month. Made by Google.

How to start
  1. Open Gemini.
  2. Ask about routes, drive times or flights.
Try saying this:How long is the drive from Chicago to Nashville, and where's a nice place to stop halfway?
Open Google Gemini

Ranked by: does the job well, easy to use, good value, and trustworthy. No company can pay for a spot.
