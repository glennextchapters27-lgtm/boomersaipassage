Write a letter or email

Complaint, thank-you, hard conversation

Short answer: Our top pick is Claude. Good at matching your tone, whether firm, friendly or formal. Tell it who the letter is for and what you want to happen.

Our picks, best first

1

Claude

Best for careful, warm writing

Good at matching your tone, whether firm, friendly or formal. Tell it who the letter is for and what you want to happen.

Easy to use ●●●●○ · Value ●●●●○

Free version. Pro about $20 a month. Made by Anthropic.

How to start
  1. Go to claude.ai and make a free account.
  2. Type what happened and who the letter is for.
  3. Ask for changes in plain words: "shorter", "less angry", "more polite".
Try saying this:Help me write a polite but firm letter to my cable company. I was charged $40 twice in October and want a refund. Keep it under 150 words.
Open Claude
2

ChatGPT

Best all-rounder

Fast and easy. The phone app lets you talk instead of type.

Easy to use ●●●●● · Value ●●●●○

Free version. Paid plans from about $8 a month; Plus about $20. Made by OpenAI.

How to start
  1. Download the ChatGPT app or go to chatgpt.com.
  2. Tap the microphone and say what you want to write.
  3. Copy the result into your email.
Try saying this:Write a short thank-you note to my neighbor who shoveled my driveway all week.
Open ChatGPT
3

Google Gemini

Best if you use Gmail

Works inside Gmail, so it can draft replies right where you read your email.

Easy to use ●●●●○ · Value ●●●●●

Free version. Paid plans from about $5 a month. Made by Google.

How to start
  1. Open Gmail on a computer.
  2. Look for the Gemini star icon when you reply.
  3. Describe the reply you want.
Try saying this:Draft a reply saying I can't make Tuesday but Thursday afternoon works.
Open Google Gemini
