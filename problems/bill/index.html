Understand a medical bill or insurance letter

What does this actually say?

Short answer: Our top pick is Claude. You can upload a photo or PDF of the letter and ask it to explain each line in plain English.

Our picks, best first

1

Claude

Best for long, confusing documents

You can upload a photo or PDF of the letter and ask it to explain each line in plain English.

Easy to use ●●●●○ · Value ●●●●○

Free version. Pro about $20 a month. Made by Anthropic.

How to start
  1. Black out your member ID and Social Security number first.
  2. Upload a photo of the page at claude.ai.
  3. Ask: what do I owe, and is anything wrong?
Try saying this:Explain this Medicare letter in simple words. What do I owe, by when, and is there anything I should question?

2

ChatGPT

Easiest from your phone

Snap a photo in the app and ask questions about it.

Easy to use ●●●●● · Value ●●●●○

Free version. Paid plans from about $8 a month; Plus about $20. Made by OpenAI.

How to start
  1. Open the ChatGPT app.
  2. Tap the + button and take a photo of the bill.
  3. Ask it to explain the charges.
Try saying this:Which of these charges should insurance have covered? Give me questions to ask the billing office.
