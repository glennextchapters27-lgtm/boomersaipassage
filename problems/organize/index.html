Organize my week and reminders

Calendar, to-dos, pills

Short answer: Our top pick is Google Gemini. Can add events and reminders to your Google Calendar when you ask out loud.

Our picks, best first

1

Google Gemini

Best on Android and with Google Calendar

Can add events and reminders to your Google Calendar when you ask out loud.

Easy to use ●●●●○ · Value ●●●●●

Free version. Paid plans from about $5 a month. Made by Google.

How to start
  1. Open Gemini on your phone.
  2. Say: remind me to...
  3. Check that it appears in your calendar.
Try saying this:Remind me every Monday at 9 a.m. to take out the recycling.
