Stay safe

Is AI safe? Yes, with five simple rules.

AI helpers are as safe as any website when you follow a few habits. Here they are, plus how to spot the new AI scams.

Short answer: Yes. Using ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini is safe if you never share passwords or account numbers, double-check health, money and legal answers, and use the official app or website.

The five rules

  1. Protect your numbers. Never type in your Social Security, bank or card numbers, or passwords.
  2. Double-check big answers. AI can be confidently wrong. Ask a real person about health, money or the law.
  3. Use the official app or website. Search for the name yourself rather than clicking a link someone sent you.
  4. Start free. Pay only after a tool has helped you for a few weeks.
  5. Hurry plus money means stop. If a message, call or "AI" asks for money or gift cards in a hurry, it's a scam pattern.

The new AI scams to know

  • Voice cloning: a call that sounds like a grandchild in trouble. Hang up and call them back on their real number.
  • Deepfake videos: a famous person "recommending" an investment. Real ones don't.
  • Fake AI apps: copycats with similar names. Check who made the app before you download it.

Common questions

Is it safe for seniors to use ChatGPT?
Yes, if you never share passwords or account numbers and double-check important health, money or legal answers with a real person.

What is an AI voice-cloning scam?
A scammer copies a loved one's voice from short recordings and calls asking for money in a hurry. Hang up and call the person back on the number you know.

Can AI tell me if a text is a scam?
It can point out common warning signs. Paste the text into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini without clicking any link, and ask.
