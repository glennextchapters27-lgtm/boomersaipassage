AI word list, in plain English

Accessibility
Features that help with eyesight, hearing or hands, like bigger text and read-aloud.
Like reading glasses for your phone.
Agent
An AI that does a series of steps for you, not just answers a question.
Like an assistant you send on errands.
AI
Artificial intelligence. A computer program that can understand and write everyday language.
Like a very well-read assistant.
App
A program you install on your phone or tablet.
Like a tool in your toolbox.
Browser
The program you use to visit websites: Chrome, Safari, Edge.
Like the window you look at the internet through.
Chatbot
An AI you talk with by typing or speaking. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are chatbots.
Like texting a helpful expert.
ChatGPT
The best-known chatbot, made by a company called OpenAI.
One brand of AI helper.
Claude
A chatbot made by a company called Anthropic. Known for careful writing.
One brand of AI helper.
Copilot
Microsoft's chatbot. Built into Windows and Word.
Microsoft's AI helper.
Deepfake
A fake video, photo or voice made with AI to look or sound real.
Like a very convincing forgery.
Free plan
The no-cost version of a tool. It usually has daily limits.
Like a free sample.
Gemini
Google's chatbot. Built into Gmail and Android phones.
Google's AI helper.
Hallucination
When AI makes something up but sounds sure. Always double-check important facts.
Like a confident guess that's wrong.
Image generator
An AI that draws pictures from a description you type.
Like an artist who works from your words.
Link
Words or a button you tap to go to a web page. Don't tap links from strangers.
Like a door to another room.
Log in
Signing in to your account with your email and password.
Like unlocking your front door.
Memory
When a chatbot remembers things about you from earlier chats. You can turn it off.
Like a helper who remembers your preferences.
Model
The "brain" inside a chatbot. Companies release new models every few months.
Like a car's engine. Same car, newer engine.
Perplexity
An AI search tool that shows the websites it got its answer from.
Like a librarian who cites sources.
Privacy
Who can see what you type. Don't type in passwords, account or Social Security numbers.
Like what you'd say on a postcard.
Project
A folder in a chatbot that keeps related chats and files together.
Like a file folder for one topic.
Prompt
Whatever you type or say to the AI. Your question or request.
Like telling a helper what you need.
Response
The AI's answer to your prompt.
Like the reply to a letter.
Scam
A trick to get your money or information. AI makes scams more convincing.
Like an old con with new tools.
Screenshot
A picture of what's on your screen. Useful for showing the AI a problem.
Like photocopying your screen.
Subscription
A monthly fee for more use or better features. You can usually cancel any time.
Like a magazine subscription.
Summarize
Asking AI to shorten something long into the main points.
Like asking for the CliffsNotes.
Two-step verification
A second check, like a code texted to you, when you log in. It keeps others out.
Like a deadbolt on top of the lock.
Upload
Sending a photo or file from your phone or computer to the AI so it can look at it.
Like handing someone a document.
Vibe coding
Building a simple app or web page by describing it in plain words. No programming needed.
Like telling a carpenter what you want built.
Voice cloning
Copying someone's voice with AI. Scammers use it to fake calls from family.
Like an impersonator. If a call asks for money, hang up and call back.
Voice mode
Talking to the AI out loud instead of typing. It talks back.
Like a phone call with a helper.
