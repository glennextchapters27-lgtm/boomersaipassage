Step-by-step guide

How to use ChatGPT, in plain English.

Five minutes, no tech skills. What you need, how to start for free, and what to type first.

Short answer: Go to chatgpt.com on any phone, tablet or computer, type a question in the box at the bottom, and press Enter. The free version is enough to start.

What you need

  • The phone, tablet or computer you already own
  • Your regular internet
  • An email address, if you want it to save your chats (optional)

Start in 4 steps

  1. Type chatgpt.com into your web browser. Or download the "ChatGPT" app made by OpenAI. Check the maker's name, because copycat apps exist.
  2. Click in the box at the bottom.
  3. Type your question in plain words, like you'd ask a helpful neighbor.
  4. Press Enter. Read the answer. Ask a follow-up if you want more.
Your first questionI'm new to AI. In simple words, what are 5 useful things you could help me with this week?

Good things to try next

Write a letterHelp me write a polite letter to my insurance company asking why my claim was denied. Here are the details: [details]
Explain somethingExplain what an annuity is in simple words, like you're talking to a smart friend who isn't a finance person.
Check a messageIs this text a scam? What are the warning signs? [paste the text, but don't click any link in it]

What it costs

The free version handles everyday questions. Paid plans add more use and features; start free and pay only if it has helped you for a few weeks.

Stay safe

  • Never type in your Social Security, bank or card numbers, or passwords.
  • It can be confidently wrong. Double-check anything about health, money or the law with a real person.

Prefer a different helper? Claude (claude.ai) and Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) work the same way. See our picks.

Common questions

Is ChatGPT free?
Yes, there's a free version that handles everyday questions. Paid plans are optional.

Do I need to download anything?
No. You can use it in your web browser at chatgpt.com. There's also an official app from OpenAI.

Is ChatGPT safe for seniors?
It's safe to use if you follow two rules: never share passwords or account numbers, and double-check important health, money or legal answers with a real person.
